COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we start.

How much money should I set aside to get started?

Budget for the ads themselves, our monthly service charge, and any one-time setup fee. For example, if you set aside KSh 30,000 for ads, your first-month total is KSh 30,000 plus the service charge and any setup fee in your quote. We’ll show you that total before you approve the work.

What does the monthly service charge cover?

It pays us to plan, write, and manage your ads, check which ones bring in calls or orders, and explain what we’d change next. You pay the service charge to us. Your quote spells out the work and the monthly fee.

Who do I pay for the ads themselves?

Meta bills you for Facebook and Instagram ads, and Google bills you for Google ads. The charges go through the payment method on your own ad account. We agree on the ad budget with you before the ads go live.

When would I pay a setup fee?

A one-time setup fee can apply if we need to set up a new ad account or fix how calls, bookings, or orders are counted. We’ll explain the work and show the charge in your quote so you can approve it first.

Will you ask for my ad account passwords?

No. You invite us to your Meta or Google Ads account and give us the access needed to manage the ads. Keep your passwords and verification codes private. You stay in control and can remove our access.

How do I engage Sephanly?

Request a quote by WhatsApp or email. Tell us what you sell, where your customers are, and roughly what you can spend. We’ll discuss where to advertise and send a written quote in KSh covering the ad budget, service charge, and any setup fee. Work starts after you approve it.

Do I need to advertise everywhere?

You can start with Facebook and Instagram or with Google. We’ll help you decide where your budget has the best chance of bringing in customers.

Do I keep ownership of my ad accounts?

Yes. The accounts and billing stay in your name. You give us access to manage the ads.

What if I’ve never run ads before?

Tell us what you sell, where customers can find you, and roughly what you’d like to spend. We can help you set up your first ads.

Will I know if the ads are working?

We’ll agree on what matters to your business, such as paid orders, booked jobs, or serious enquiries. We’ll review those numbers with you alongside what you spent.
